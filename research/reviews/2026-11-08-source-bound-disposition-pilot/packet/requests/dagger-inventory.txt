Dagger provides: a complete execution engine and system API; SDKs for 8 languages; an interactive REPL; a rich ecosystem of reusable modules; and more.\n0016| \n0017| **Local-first**. Once you automate a task with Dagger, it will reliably run on any supported system: your laptop, AI sandbox, CI server, or dedicated cloud infrastructure. Debug complex workflows immediately instead of guessing what went wrong from a wall of text logs.\n0022| \n0023| ## Features\n0024| \n0025| **System API**.
