# Direct technical prose: contextual interpretation of existing policy

This is an editorial profile, not an authorship test or a requirement that the
original text be unintelligible. A needed revision is a wording change justified
by this profile. A warning can preserve a technically correct proposition while
removing an independently identifiable burden. Do not reject a finding merely
because the passage performs a useful function or because its facts are correct.
Do not accept a finding merely because an alternative is shorter.

The following public Unswell rule guidance supplies the policy anchors. The
general contextual tests are hypotheses, not claims that the pattern matcher
already detects every related construction.

1. `filler.instruction-scaffolding`: "State the supported action directly;
   preserve its conditions and optionality." "Remove narrated setup or nested
   support layers around the action and its method." Inspect separate clauses
   inside a unit. State the actual action/relation/format/role and then identify
   any additional prose layer between its actor and payload. A legitimate
   prerequisite stays; a separate narrated method introduction need not stay.
   Distinguish a behavior definition from a support assertion about that same
   behavior. Retain the named operation, actor, operand, input type and modality.

2. `filler.nominal-support`: "Consider expressing the action or frequency
   without this support noun." Keep actions and distinctions, but examine a
   nominal carrier plus a second action or relation that supplies its actual
   meaning. Repeated technical terms are allowed; two genuinely different
   definition/type levels must survive. Do not equate every nominalization,
   passive predicate, role definition or statement of capability with padding.

3. `filler.evaluative-closure`: "This clause announces information or endorses
   a result; state the information directly." An importance, helpfulness,
   intended-use or purpose announcement can wrap useful content. Assess whether
   the wrapper adds a separately supported qualification, or whether the
   concrete requirement, use or consequence already carries its useful meaning.
   Keep uncertainty, conditions and strength of obligation. Do not silently
   convert advice, usefulness, aspiration or an attempt into a guarantee.

4. `repetition.redundant-predicate`: "The predicate repeats the relation
   already named by its subject." `repetition.definition-echo`: "State the
   definition or distinction once. Preserve modifiers, negation and limits."
   Ask what new fact a definition adds in its full context. Preserve genuine
   binding of a named state, role, term or identifier to behavior. A dictionary
   synonym or circular term description can be a burden when the informative
   relation can be stated directly. Ordinary explanatory definitions stay when
   this specific burden is not demonstrated. A name alone is not proof that
   every reader already knows its operational meaning.

5. `filler.unscoped-assurance`: "State the behavior, scope or evidence behind
   this quality claim." Separate reported behavior or a locally explained
   tradeoff from an added degree, vague judgment, aspiration or closing claim.
   Preserve attribution and relevant uncertainty. An unsupported degree can be
   diagnosed without demanding measurements from every ordinary description.

6. `filler.document-justification`: "State the scope or destination directly
   instead of explaining why this page exists." Keep useful orientation,
   boundaries, ownership, references and point-of-action safety reminders.
   Examine whether the actual scope or behavior is being announced again through
   authorial process, exposition order or a ceremonial closing summary.

Independent safeguards:

- Correct grammar does not exempt a separate profile-relevant burden. Incorrect
  grammar does not complete the review of other clauses in the same unit.
- A valid passive or conventional role definition needs no warning merely for
  being passive. Identify the actual nested/repeated/support layer and show what
  remains after addressing it. If only personal wording preference remains,
  emit nothing.
- A concrete actor, role, accepted format, prerequisite, safety condition,
  algorithmic reason or technical distinction is useful content, not padding.
  Its presence does not prove every surrounding clause adds useful content.
- Diagnose a referent or technical inconsistency only when the page establishes
  it; use verification-only advice when the replacement fact is unknown.
- Treat ordinary English, punctuation, code, numerals, headings and long
  sentences as evidence to inspect, never as proof of machine authorship.

Before deciding to retain an original, identify its useful proposition and the
strongest reason to retain the exact criticized layer. Before issuing a warning,
identify that layer, its specific burden, and what must survive. The same
standard applies to grammar and rhetoric. No checklist receipt or self-approval
substitutes for the separate frozen editorial evaluation.
