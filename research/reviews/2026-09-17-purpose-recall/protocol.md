## Hypotheses fixed before selecting new pages

Expand the existing bounded rhetorical families rather than adding keyword bans:

- Purpose and relevance tails use an anaphoric subject or repeated gerund action,
  a copula, and a relative-purpose complement: what a named feature/verb/refusal
  is for/about, the fact a feature exists for, or the question/part that matters.
  Require the clause relation, not just a word such as purpose. Keep the concrete
  behavior, conditions, source link and any useful scope definition in the advice.
  Actual named actions, mechanisms, actor distinctions and requirements remain
  controls; an arbitrary component having a purpose is not enough.
- Information-value and verification announcements can place their cognitive
  predicate after a nominal complement, introduce an abstract intentionality
  claim, or portray output as proof of a tool's understanding. Distinguish a
  claim about presentation or understanding from a concrete measured property.
  Do not flag a cost-benefit comparison, quoted claim, negation or instruction
  to verify a specific condition simply because it contains an evaluation word.
- Reader preference and prevalence claims use generic people/users/production
  environments with unsupported majority/frequency language and a preference,
  intention or default-practice predicate. A popularity claim is not justified
  merely by a colon followed by a recommendation. Preserve quantified surveys,
  reported evidence, technical universal invariants, permission statements and
  explicitly scoped uncertainty. No isolated word is a diagnostic.
- Document announcements declare what a section will describe or look at, often
  through a nominalized or future predicate. Preserve useful navigation, links,
  concrete scope boundaries and document contents. Report removable presentation
  scaffolding with advice to keep that content; do not infer that a whole scope
  paragraph can be deleted.

Also test whether sentence-wide guards hide independently identifiable local
frames. A number, condition or reason elsewhere in the sentence must not erase
an otherwise supported editorial construction. Bound the actual candidate span;
keep the rule's existing work budget and avoid unbounded scans of long clauses.
Preserve operational qualifiers in findings and editing guidance. Questions,
quotations, code boundaries, source mappings and legitimate contrasts remain
controls. Any changed rule gets a version change, without lower thresholds or
higher weights. Unsupported semantic judgments stay missed or uncertain.
