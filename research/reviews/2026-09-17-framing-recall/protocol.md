# Contextual framing recall, issue 299

The development set is the frozen September 17 whole-page audit. Selection of
nine confirmation sources preceded implementation. One page per Ptah
format/length cell and one historical page per length cell is selected by SHA-256
of `issue299-confirmation-v1\n` plus the pinned reference URL, after excluding all
26 development pages. Keep the selected pages even if no targeted defect occurs.
The prior audit's frame supplies lengths and source identities, never outcomes.

Target three bounded construction families before writing matchers:

- Anaphoric evaluation of an explanation: whole-point/design declarations,
  bare evaluative tails, and abstract measured-versus-assumed assurances.
  Preserve a named purpose, a concrete measurement and the conditions of a claim.
- A document part praised as earning its place. Recognize the document relation,
  not arbitrary uses of the word column or legitimate layout specifications.
- Unscoped self-certification: intentional quality announcements without a
  mechanism, nearly-always-right assertions, and promises that something is the
  fastest way to understand everything. Reject numeric scopes, conditions,
  negations, questions, protected constructions and operational guarantees.

Use existing tokens, block boundaries, candidate budgets and source segments.
Retain the current gates and thresholds. New diagnostics name their matched
construction and ask for behavior, scope or evidence; they cannot establish that
an assertion is factually false. Do not require all development misses to match.

The same Codex assistant reviews complete confirmation sources for these three
families after implementation and before opening their diagnostic outputs. This
is an exposed reviewer, not an independent or blinded experiment. Record exact
byte spans, rationale, edits, controls, unresolved cases and complete source
coverage. Imported components and images are not expanded. Do not infer quality
or human origin from a historical snapshot. After outputs are opened, freeze the
implementation for this confirmation run; subsequent tuning needs new inputs.

Compare both technical and strict profiles, using exact source hashes. Publish
every added/removed diagnostic and its disposition on development and confirmation
pages, frozen-event recall on the development set, and targeted-event recall on
the confirmation set. Preserve uncertain judgments; exclude them from positives.
Use semantic compatibility and source overlap for credit, not warning counts.
Report resource costs and all negative regressions. The maintainer accepts this
review method under ADR 0041; no independent human reviewer is required.
