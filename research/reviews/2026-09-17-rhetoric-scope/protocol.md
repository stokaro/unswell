# Clause scope and information evaluation

This iteration follows the exposed 106-page review through PR #319. Its baseline
is commit 170135f888b16e6f1ee1b922141e6ab37ea5bb85. The goal remains broad useful
recall, with working objectives of 80% frozen-event recall and 85% soft diagnostic
precision. A small gain on familiar pages does not establish that goal.

## Engineering hypotheses fixed before confirmation

1. Attribution and measurement guards must apply to the clause they qualify.
   An introductory value declaration must not disappear because a later
   operational clause says what a probe reports. Quoted and reported judgments
   must remain protected, including an attribution before a colon or dash.
   A condition or measurement attached to the judgment must remain in scope.
2. Information evaluations form bounded grammatical constructions. Recognize
   deictic/discourse subjects declaring importance; cognitive-worth predicates
   with limited manner/frequency qualifiers; and abstract whole-value or
   whole-purpose announcements. Require a complete predicate, preserve its
   operands, and distinguish concrete operational purposes and costs.
   Local relative tails must keep their antecedent and real source boundaries.
3. Instruction projection must start at a grammatical subject. An imperative
   changelog entry, issue-link residue and an upload-form relative definition
   must not be treated as a reader instruction. Generic feature-introduction
   uncertainty remains visible rather than being assumed resolved.

The targets come from frozen exposed events, including development p001-d03 and
p007-d03; exposed-local c02-d02; and exposed-projection c02-d02/c02-d05/c03-d01.
All other exposed misses remain in the denominator. This is a change to the
existing bounded engine, not an authorship classifier or a semantic equivalence
claim. Do not lower gates or introduce isolated-word bans. Required qualifiers,
identifiers, numbers, negation and protected operands must survive proposed edits.

## Source and annotation freeze

The selector ranks pinned source identities with seed
`rhetoric-scope-confirmation-v1`. Exclude all 106 exposed references and hashes
and the original historical study. Select one new short, medium and long page
per cohort, with distinct historical repositories. Record shortages explicitly.
Freeze the protocol, selector, source manifest and source archive before reading
the selected prose. Read every extracted prose block and table cell, with source
context, before runtime changes or diagnostic output. Freeze all seven editorial
categories, source spans, rationale, repairs, uncertainties and acceptable controls.

The implementing Codex assistant is the actual reviewer accepted under ADR 0041.
No independent human or native-speaker review is claimed or required. Unknown
origin stays unknown. Historical material is not automatically human authored.

## Evaluation and stopping rule

Replay both profiles on all exposed pages and the new complete pages. Retain
source integrity, explicit binary identities, JSON output and resource records.
Publish every added, removed or replaced finding with its reviewer disposition.
An overlapping length or punctuation finding does not detect a wording defect.
Partial detections stay partial; post-diagnostic discoveries cannot earn frozen
recall credit. Existing restricted annotation scopes remain explicit.

Open confirmation outputs only after semantic edits and focused regressions are
frozen. Further semantic tuning makes those pages exposed and requires another
source freeze. Report all misses, false positives, uncertainties, abstentions and
operational failures even if the candidate is unsuccessful. One page per cell
provides development counts, not population estimates or independent qualification.

Run public blackbox regressions, source-mapping and condition controls, ordinary
required checks, resource bounds and self-checks. Race, fuzz and coverage remain
deferred under #123. No merge, release, deployment or change to default gates is
part of this protocol.
