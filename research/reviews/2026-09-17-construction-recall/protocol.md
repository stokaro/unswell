## Hypotheses fixed before confirmation

The exposed pages repeatedly use these grammatical constructions:

- Cognitive-worth and notice announcements: a subject introduces information
  as worth knowing, stating or mentioning; impersonal modal passives announce
  that information should or can be noted. Detect the announcement clause,
  preserving an attached reason, condition, quantity or instruction. A concrete
  action being worth its cost is different from announcing information.
- Indirect methods: an anaphoric or nominalized action is done, performed or
  achieved by an explicit method. State the action and method without the
  generic doing frame. Keep agents, alternatives and operational conditions.
- Reader intentions and capabilities: optional intent introduces a supported
  action or an instruction. Use a purpose clause or direct capability statement.
  Real input-state, permission, failure and availability limits remain controls.
  Expand syntactic coverage, including embedded capability clauses, rather than
  diagnosing standalone modal verbs or changing optionality into an obligation.
- Repeated relational definitions: a subject names a relation and the predicate
  repeats that relation without distinguishing an operand. Require grammatical
  roles and unchanged referents; ordinary controllers, technical identities,
  mappings and differently qualified operands remain controls.
- Document maintenance: an explicit document or its material is listed or
  repeated here, or omitted to prevent restating another source. Diagnose the
  editorial justification, preserving the source link and actual scope boundary.

Use existing rule families where their meaning agrees, with explicit version
changes. Reuse extraction, NLP, source maps, bounded windows and resource policy.
Do not infer semantic equivalence from shared nouns, classify authorship, lower
thresholds or change gates. A condition elsewhere in a sentence should not hide
an independently identifiable announcement; the diagnostic and suggested edit
must preserve that condition. Unsupported constructions remain reported misses.
