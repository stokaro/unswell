# #421: bounded offline evidence decision

This is a read-only decision record for [#421](https://github.com/stokaro/unswell/issues/421), related to but not a completion claim for #349. It replays the published v30 detector and the saved candidate/selection evidence on the unchanged 36 source pages. It makes **no model calls**, changes no detector, opens no prospective page, and does not reinterpret a location overlap as a complete editorial diagnosis.

## Decision

**No quality gain.** Suppressing the whole `syntax.long-sentence` family removes 45 uncertain criticisms but also 52 accepted ones and the sole published witness for `whole/p026-d02`. The remaining whole-page stream is 25/37 accepted (67.6%) with 21/57 complete events. The original whole-page stream was 69/121 accepted (57.0%) with 22/57 complete events. Neither reaches the unchanged 85% accepted or 80% complete-event target. The ablation is a diagnostic control, **not** a proposed default.

No source-grounded restriction was executed. The retained structural observations show that word count and simple finite-verb/subordination counts overlap between accepted and uncertain cases; they do not justify a rule that distinguishes necessary splitting from coherent technical lists. The whole-page family contains 44 accepted and 40 uncertain findings. If this family alone were restricted, reaching 85% whole-page usefulness while retaining the other 37 deliveries would require removing **all 40** uncertain family findings while losing at most **one** accepted family finding; `whole/p026-d02` must survive. That is an optimistic arithmetic bound, not a trained or tested policy. A threshold sweep would repeat an already negative experiment.

The complete-claim pool is genuinely complementary, but it supplies no new executable selector. The accepted original pool can support 55/57 whole, 40/46 context, and 18/20 historically exposed events; the accepted subset with assessed safe or not-applicable advice supports 55/57, 38/46, and 18/20. The separated-judgment stream delivers 48/57, 39/46, and 17/20 complete events, but its whole-page accepted fraction is 1,627/2,101 (77.4%) and five supplied suggestions remain unresolved. It loses two events the v30 detector had completed. No deterministic source-grounded policy distinct from the already negative source-anchor, committee, source-aware logistic, and semantic-neighbor selectors is supported by these artifacts. The cached-pool branch stops here; a retrospective oracle is not a product input.

**Selected continuation:** pursue the bounded task-mapping and unchanged-control screen in [#365](https://github.com/stokaro/unswell/issues/365) before training a revision-benefit transfer model. The causal hypothesis is that before/after edits may teach the necessity of a source-side change better than admitting a prewritten criticism. The next fixed comparator is the unchanged v30 and separated-stream accounting above. First, use at most eight eligible, source-distinct TETRA paper groups and at most 32 passages, including edited examples and acceptable unchanged controls; keep quarantined groups excluded. Review editorial preference, necessity, and factual preservation as separate axes. This is an offline mapping screen with zero new model calls and zero expected Unswell event or finding delta. Stop before any fit if an imported identity or source span fails verification, or if fewer than six groups yield both a usable edited example and an acceptable unchanged control with distinct necessity judgments. Record missing examples rather than substituting another group after seeing the judgments. If this passes, preregister one pairwise baseline and resource cap before fitting. Its eventual comparison must use the original 57/46/20 event denominators, all delivered findings, the v30 control, and the separated-stream comparator. An improvement sufficient for #349 would require at least +24/+26/+12 FULL events over v30 in the three cohorts, at least 85% accepted actual deliveries in each, complete availability, and zero unsafe or unresolved advice; any smaller gain is reported as a measured frontier change only. A TETRA-only score will not count. Python/model research remains isolated from the pure-Go product. Any eventual product proposal needs bounded source-only inference through the public engine, original byte spans and protected regions, a Go-export/parity proof if a model is used, and the existing performance limits. This continuation does not solve #349 yet.

## Reproduction and limits

Run `verify.py` with `--v30-root`, `--source-manifest`, `--source-root`, and `--output`; run `pool.py` with the expanded original/grade packet, the sealed separated evaluation, the public separated evaluation and manifest, the v30 event review, the same source manifest/root, and `--output`. Both programs are read-only except for their declared output file. Local private source paths are supplied as arguments and are not retained in outputs. [`ablation.json`](ablation.json) and [`loss-ledger.json`](loss-ledger.json) retain input digests. The six negative tests in `test_verify.py` and `test_pool.py` reject source/hash drift, missing pages, changed denominators, unsupported FULL credit, incompatible source joins, changed grades, hidden deliveries, and attempted policy export from the retrospective oracle. Run them with `python3 -B -m unittest discover -s research/reviews/2026-11-03-issue421 -p 'test_*.py'`.

This study has **no quality gain** and no current-main detector qualification. The v30 record is the published, editor-reviewed checkpoint named by #421; the later exact-main CLI replay has 431 findings but only an optimistic source-location audit, not a complete current-main acceptance review. The future screen in #365 must establish its own frozen inputs, independent source groups, and unchanged controls before any training or prospective confirmation. #349's 80% FULL / 85% accepted targets and untouched confirmation remain open.
