# #421: bounded offline evidence decision

This is a read-only decision record for [#421](https://github.com/stokaro/unswell/issues/421), related to but not a completion claim for #349. It replays the published v30 detector and the saved candidate/selection evidence on the unchanged 36 source pages. It makes **no model calls**, changes no detector, opens no prospective page, and does not reinterpret a location overlap as a complete editorial diagnosis.

## Decision

**No quality gain.** Suppressing the whole `syntax.long-sentence` family removes 45 uncertain criticisms but also 52 accepted ones and the sole published witness for `whole/p026-d02`. The remaining whole-page stream is 25/37 accepted (67.6%) with 21/57 complete events. The original whole-page stream was 69/121 accepted (57.0%) with 22/57 complete events. Neither reaches the unchanged 85% accepted or 80% complete-event target. The ablation is a diagnostic control, **not** a proposed default.

No source-grounded restriction was executed. The retained structural observations show that word count and simple finite-verb/subordination counts overlap between accepted and uncertain cases; they do not justify a rule that distinguishes necessary splitting from coherent technical lists. The whole-page family contains 44 accepted and 40 uncertain findings. If this family alone were restricted, reaching 85% whole-page usefulness while retaining the other 37 deliveries would require removing **all 40** uncertain family findings while losing at most **one** accepted family finding; `whole/p026-d02` must survive. That is an optimistic arithmetic bound, not a trained or tested policy. A threshold sweep would repeat an already negative experiment.

The complete-claim pool is genuinely complementary, but it supplies no new executable selector. The accepted original pool can support 55/57 whole, 40/46 context, and 18/20 historically exposed events; the accepted subset with assessed safe or not-applicable advice supports 55/57, 38/46, and 18/20. The separated-judgment stream delivers 48/57, 39/46, and 17/20 complete events, but its whole-page accepted fraction is 1,627/2,101 (77.4%) and five supplied suggestions remain unresolved. It loses two events the v30 detector had completed. No deterministic source-grounded policy distinct from the already negative source-anchor, committee, source-aware logistic, and semantic-neighbor selectors is supported by these artifacts. The cached-pool branch stops here; a retrospective oracle is not a product input.

**Selected continuation:** make one bounded, source-only necessity-support audit for the long-sentence family under #349. The causal hypothesis is that a source-visible discourse or technical dependency, rather than sentence length or shallow finite-tag counts, could distinguish a necessary split from a coherent technical list. Freeze up to six accepted/uncertain matched pairs from the existing 97 reviewed findings, with the sole FULL witness for `whole/p026-d02` included. Hide grades and benchmark event IDs while recording candidate source features; preserve the original judgments and inspect the complete source, quantities, conditions, and protected regions. This is a feasibility audit, with no new model call, parser, fit, product change, or expected event/finding delta, and a one-workday resource cap. If no single source-visible relation can separate at least five of the six pairs without excluding the sole FULL witness, stop this family rather than tune thresholds. If one can, freeze exactly one restriction before evaluating it against the unchanged v30 stream and all 57/46/20 event denominators; accept a frontier gain only if the actual delivered accepted fraction improves without losing FULL events, availability, or advice safety in any cohort. This is not #349 qualification: that still needs at least +24/+26/+12 FULL events over v30, at least 85% accepted actual deliveries in each cohort, complete availability, zero unsafe or unresolved advice, and untouched prospective confirmation. Any eventual rule belongs in the public Go engine with original byte spans and protected regions. This continuation does not solve #349 yet.
