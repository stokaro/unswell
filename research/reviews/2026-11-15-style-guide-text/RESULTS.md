## What the second reading follows

The sentences of the same pages, counted after the control and not registered beforehand. A sentence counts when it
stands in a running paragraph, and it is plain by the rule of the build: five to twenty words, and none of `? ! ( ) ; :` and
the em dash among what a reader sees of it. Each figure is the median over the pages of a group; a page with fewer than
10 such sentences is left out.

| Pages | Number | Words per sentence | Sentences over 20 words | Plain sentences | Plain sentences in runs | In runs by chance | Runs to chance | Paragraph sentences per 1,000 words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Style-guide set | 30 | 12.5 | 7.9% | 68.1% | 58.3% | 52.0% | 1.07 | 71.8 |
| Matched articles | 128 | 17.6 | 32.4% | 47.6% | 30.6% | 26.8% | 1.09 | 49.5 |
| Matched documentation | 48 | 17.1 | 28.5% | 53.8% | 37.5% | 32.1% | 1.11 | 51.0 |

"In runs by chance" is what the same page would have if its plain sentences stood at random places among its sentences.
"Runs to chance" divides the plain sentences in runs of a whole group by that. It is close to one in every group: a page has
about as many sentences in runs as its share of plain sentences gives. The second reading is in effect a measure of how many
sentences of a page are short plain statements, for each 1,000 words.

The average length of a sentence alone tells the style-guide set from the matched pages with an AUC of 0.933, and the share of plain sentences with 0.900. The second reading gives 0.939 against the same pages.

28 of the 176 matched pages have at least as many plain sentences as the lower quartile of the style-guide set, 61.8%. Their sentences still have 15.3 words at the median, their second readings run from 9 to 71, and 1 of them is `high`.

The row of the style-guide set is counted on its posts and is not recounted here: the record holds nothing of a post but its
readings. The sentences of the other pages are rebuilt from the result of the build. The rebuilt count of sentences in runs equals the build's own on 329 of 339 pages, 6,585 against 6,595 in all.
