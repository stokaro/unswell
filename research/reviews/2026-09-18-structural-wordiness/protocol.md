# Structural wordiness: development hypotheses

Written before selecting or reading a new confirmation sample and before runtime
changes. The 19 existing sets are exposed development evidence. Their 801 missed
full events include partial detections; an absent full-event credit is not proof
that no relevant warning was emitted.

Primary families to test together:

1. Local support constructions that wrap a concrete action: possibility/ability,
   nominal use, nominal manner or periodicity, and procedural support. Preserve
   the actor, modal force, conditions, quantities, operands and method. Distinguish
   an actual responsibility assignment, permission or possible failure from
   narrated instructions. Use source-bound construction evidence, not a banned
   word count. Never turn a capability into a command.
2. Subject clefts whose predicate is an action (X is what VERB; which is what
   VERB). A what-clause with its own subject is an object/content clause and a
   control. Explicit contrast, quoted assertions, equations and terminology
   definitions require separate controls. Preserve focus when it distinguishes
   actual technical alternatives.
3. Repeated grammatical support in one clause (also ... as well; like ... for
   example; and then finally). Recognize the relationship and its scope, and
   preserve distinct coordinated meanings, chronology and quoted language.
4. An action announcement and its immediately anaphoric method can span adjacent
   prose blocks in the same section. Extend the existing ownership relation to
   nominal methods where warranted; do not join arbitrary paragraphs or cross
   headings, unrelated lists, examples, or source files. A partial local warning
   is not a full diagnosis of a multi-part annotated editing operation.

Use shared extraction, NLP, budgets, mapping and report contracts. Reuse existing
rules where the message still describes the editing operation; introduce a rule
only when it names a distinct operation. Do not change gate thresholds or promote
severity to manufacture failures. Do not claim semantic equivalence from lexical
similarity or use origin as the defect label.

The next step is a metadata-only availability audit of unused source identities.
Choose and freeze the confirmation allocation before manual source review. Prior
long pages remain mandatory regression inputs even if no unused long Ptah pages
remain. Do not label a new version of an exposed page as independent confirmation.
