# Repeated propositions and explanatory redundancy

This iteration starts from PR #320, commit
f67de48d45288cffdb4609f04ed35d3d1bacbc79. Its 112 exposed pages remain development
evidence. The product objective remains useful broad wording detection, with
working objectives of 80% frozen-event recall and 85% soft diagnostic precision.
A gain on one construction or one familiar page does not satisfy that objective.

## Hypotheses before confirmation

Use the existing extraction, surface NLP, source maps and bounded rule pipeline.
Investigate related propositions, preserving their actors and restrictions:

1. An explicit exclusivity restriction may be restated by negating its complement.
   Require matching actors, relations and properties. A restatement marker is
   context, not proof. Preserve modality, quantifiers, direct/transitive scope,
   conditions, exceptions, numbers and protected operands. A different actor,
   restriction or scope must not collapse into the same proposition.
2. Copular identities may repeat a nominal or gerund proposition without adding
   information. Require an actual repeated constituent; do not treat similar
   nouns, type assertions, comparisons, state persistence or useful contrasts
   as equal merely because they share vocabulary.
3. An explanation may repeat the same unqualified scalar judgment as its reason.
   Distinguish circular editorial setup from a concrete causal mechanism or a
   legitimate propagation of a property between components. Preserve any actual
   cause and identify every part required to repair the frozen event.

These hypotheses come from the exposed #305 examples and the 59 repetition events
across the exposed review sets. The glossary benefit restatement requires more
than lexical overlap; leave it unsupported if the analysis cannot preserve its
propositions. Do not introduce a blanket keyword rule or claim general semantic
equivalence. Record supported and unsupported constructions explicitly.

## Frozen confirmation

Rank pinned source identities with seed `proposition-repetition-confirmation-v1`.
Exclude all 112 exposed references and hashes and the original historical study.
Select one short, medium and long page per cohort, with distinct historical
repositories. Freeze protocol, selector, manifest, archive and retained notices
before reading the chosen prose. Read all extracted prose and table cells with
source context, using each source's native format. Freeze source-bound defects,
uncertainties and acceptable controls across all seven editorial categories
before runtime edits or diagnostic output.

The implementing Codex assistant is the reviewer accepted under ADR 0041. This
is development review, not human annotation, independent qualification or a
model-training corpus. Historical origin stays unknown unless established.

## Evaluation and limits

Replay both profiles on every exposed page and the new complete pages. Preserve
source integrity, rule and engine identities, JSON results and resource records.
Review every added, removed or replaced finding and every retained confirmation
finding. Incidental span overlap does not detect a defect; partial findings do
not earn full event credit. Post-diagnostic discoveries cannot expand the frozen
recall numerator. All misses and uncertainties remain visible.

Freeze semantic changes and focused regressions before opening confirmation
outputs. Later semantic tuning requires another confirmation source freeze.
Report counts by cohort and category, not a pooled population recall estimate.
Do not lower gates, weights or allowances to manufacture failing pages. Preserve
existing abstentions and operational failures explicitly.

Run public blackbox controls for source mappings, policy, clause boundaries,
negation, modals, quantities and actor identity, plus the ordinary required checks,
resource checks and CLI/MCP self-checks. Race, active fuzzing and coverage remain
deferred under #123. This protocol does not authorize merging or deployment.
