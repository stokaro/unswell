## Hypotheses fixed before new source review

1. Narrated instructions form a grammatical relation between a generic author
   (`we`, `let us`), future/invitational support and a concrete instructional or
   explanatory action with its object. Recognize action families rather than
   particular command names. Preserve capability versus obligation, conditions,
   operands, promised implementation changes and actual collective operations.
   Contact requests such as `let us know` are controls. A cognitive verb alone
   or a first-person pronoun is not a defect.
2. Reader inference and familiarity frames attach a generic reader's seeing,
   knowing or understanding to the actual proposition. A comma-delimited
   `as you can infer from ...` cue differs from a real access requirement or a
   conditional inference. Preserve the cited evidence in the editing advice;
   do not invent a conclusion or remove a prerequisite.
3. Detached author evaluations and modifier-plus-quality constructions add
   editorial emphasis rather than measured behavior. Require an attachment to
   a complete proposition or an explicitly evaluative quality predicate, not a
   banned adverb. Protect literal manner, quantification, comparisons, quoted
   material, negation, attribution and operational conditions. Announcements
   about deliberate distinctions must be separated from concrete design reasons.
4. Supporting extraction and clause boundaries are part of the experiment:
   distinguish an absent construction, a rejected grammatical role, a scoped
   technical control and an unsupported unit. Do not count a length warning as
   detection of a rhetorical defect or silently widen unsupported analysis.

These hypotheses target repeated families in the full exposed inventory. The
implementation may revise shared clause logic and existing rules and add a
separate diagnostic if its message names a distinct editing operation. Reuse
source-preserving extraction and POS enrichment; do not describe POS patterns
as dependency parsing. No online inference, new model, keyword ban, lower gate,
weight adjustment or authorship inference is authorized by this experiment.
