# Standalone numerical hooks: protocol preparation

This work addresses #337. The exposed example is “Four answers, and the right
one depends on whether you control the writes.” Its following heading names a
decision, so the existing repeated-count-heading rule intentionally does not match.
The task is an editorial construction check, not authorship attribution or a
claim that the number is factually incorrect.

## Candidate boundary

Evaluate a paragraph opening that substitutes a count and a generic choice noun
for its subject, then refers to an unnamed best/right choice. The diagnostic must
locate the rhetorical opening and retain the conditional information needed for a
meaning-preserving revision. Do not generalize from the word “four,” from a count
alone, or from the phrase “depends on.”

Separate this family from the existing two-location numbered-section finding.
An opening or heading that names its actual task, named alternatives, protocol
sizes, requirements, negation, and quoted or protected code are controls. Examples
include “two schemes: env and file,” fixed-width fields, data-race preconditions,
and named migration paths. Do not claim that a numerical warning proves a
mismatched enumeration or an unnecessary technical quantity.

The implementation must reuse the existing source maps, matcher budgets,
exemption policy, candidate observations, and gate semantics. Add blackbox and
CLI tests for meaningful repairs, source ranges, boundaries, candidate limits,
and abstention. A shipped construction must also pass the WASM consumer check.

## Confirmation selection must precede execution

Select and read new complete source-identified pages, then freeze their bytes,
licenses, metadata, target-family judgments, and controls before running the
candidate. Review is by the maintainer-accepted assistant under ADR 0041; do not
call it independent human annotation.

The prior numerical-framing pages, including the issue's missed example, are
exposed development data. The previous metadata selector is insufficient as-is:
newer studies store inputs in `confirmation-inputs.json` or `selection.json`, and
some inherit the repository identity from the parent record. A revised selector
must account for those records instead of mistaking a different JSON layout for
an unused page. Exclusion inventories are not annotations, but selected pages
and reserved prospective inputs must both be excluded.

The three prospective pages reserved for the contextual model experiment
(`operate/verify-a-release.md`, `versioned/migration-log.md`, and
`versioned/online-mode.md`) remain reserved. Do not inspect or use them for this
rule. A repository with no eligible new pages is a pool limitation; it is not a
reason to reuse exposed pages under a fresh-confirmation label.

## Reporting and acceptance

Report complete-page findings in technical and strict, all reviewed misses,
new false positives, unchanged findings, retained conditions, and the actual
confirmation denominator. Synthetic positives establish implementation behavior,
not real-world recall. If confirmation contains no positives, say so explicitly
and do not infer sensitivity from the negative controls. Publish a bounded
implementation with its limits or a supported deferral decision. Do not lower
thresholds, overwrite frozen labels, or expand the matcher after seeing
confirmation output without selecting another independent confirmation set.
