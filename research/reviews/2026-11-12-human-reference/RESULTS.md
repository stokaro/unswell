## Agreement between editors

On 54 LAMP paragraphs edited by three writers, one writer's edited sentences are measured against
another's, in both directions. A sentence that one writer edited was edited by the other in 69.1% of cases, and a
sentence that one writer left alone was edited by the other in 62.0%. The difference is +7.1 points.

Word by word, Cohen's kappa between two writers is 0.11. Of the words in paragraphs with three
writers, 11.9% are marked by all three, 31.5% by two, 34.2% by one, and 22.5% by none.

## Systems against the sentences a person edited

TPR is the share of edited sentences that a finding touches, FPR the share of unedited sentences. J is their
difference in points; a system that flags at random has J = 0 at any flagging rate.
