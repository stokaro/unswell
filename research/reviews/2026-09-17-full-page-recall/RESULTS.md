- ptah-sample: bootstrap interval 0.0%–7.4%; leave-one-page-out range 0.0%–4.5%.

A 0–0 bootstrap interval occurs when no sampled event was detected; it does **not** establish a true population recall of zero. The two exposed anchors were not randomly selected and receive no interval.
