# Diagnostics on real texts

This directory records what the editorial rules do on prose the corpus
holds, one record per study. A record names every rule that fired, the
passage that produced each finding, and a disposition: whether the rule
named something a reader would act on, measured something else, or could
not be settled by the text at hand.

These are studies of rule behavior. They qualify no rule and change no
default. Rule qualification against a real corpus is
[issue 26](https://github.com/stokaro/unswell/issues/26) and needs the
human judgments of [issue 22](https://github.com/stokaro/unswell/issues/22);
a disposition here is one reader's reading of one stratum.

A record states what it could not inspect. A rule that produces nothing on
a stratum leaves no passage to judge, and silence is reported as silence
rather than as a negative result.
