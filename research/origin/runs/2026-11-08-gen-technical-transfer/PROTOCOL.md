# GEN threshold transfer to technical prose

Date: November 8, 2026. Freeze this protocol, scorer, and input hashes before scoring either transfer corpus. This is an offline, model-free transport test of the fixed first-100-prose-token lexical-richness threshold from the [GEN screen](../2026-11-08-gen-edit-transfer/README.md). Token entropy is a prespecified secondary feature. The GEN screen's result and code are unchanged; no model or generator is called here.

## Inputs and labels

Read `research/origin/runs/2026-11-08-gen-edit-transfer/result.json` at its pinned SHA-256. The `generated/prefix100` thresholds are taken directly from that file: richness `0.9000000000000001` and entropy `0.9613090926950416`. Never recalibrate either threshold using a transfer corpus. Reuse the pinned `prose`, `WORD`, and `features` functions from `research/origin/runs/2026-11-07-richness-screen/run.py`. A score requires at least 100 extracted prose tokens. Preserve code, URL, and HTML masking behavior. Do not score full-document features as a replacement primary view.

The technical corpus is the complete public `research/generation/studies/long-prose-v3` input/output archive pair: 38 original documents from 20 repositories and 456 saved responses, evenly split between `generate` and `polish`, three model families, and two prompts per source. Verify archive bytes and SHA-256 against their wrappers, every source text hash, all request identities, and exact source-to-response association. All 228 `generate` responses form the primary machine group; the 228 `polish` responses are reported separately. The originals' historical authorship is **unknown**. Their fixed-threshold flag rate is a source-control rate, never a human false-positive rate.

The academic transfer corpus is `Jerry999/AIWritingEffect` at commit `6a45eddc021257b7c49de398c2fffa370c1d1bfa`, `dataset/t100/docs.jsonl`, 75,432,568 bytes, SHA-256 `428de968a71d64ddaf9f0d5afe7e4e295b2adeace77f57fd29c6252ae082d29c`. Its paper IDs are partitioned by the dataset's train/val/test/human_only metadata. Use the 89 test human originals, 143 test AI *rewrites*, 83 validation humans, and 400 human_only papers. Verify all 2,598 row identities and split counts, duplicate variants, and paper-disjoint splits. The source papers' pre-ChatGPT provenance is a dataset assertion, not independently verified. AI rewrites are a separate operation from GEN free generation and the long-prose `generate` arm; report them without treating their recall as the primary free-generation gate.

Both corpora were exposed in earlier Unswell studies before this transfer protocol, but the **GEN threshold was fixed without their scores**. This is a cross-corpus stress test, not untouched prospective confirmation. The 123 #349 editorial events and its reserved pages are neither read nor scored here.

## Fixed computations and decision

For each eligible original and machine variant, compare richness and entropy with the unchanged GEN thresholds using `score >= threshold`; report exact counts, variant and source eligibility, fixed-threshold flag fractions, paired wins (ties count as half), and all abstentions. Break out the technical results by operation, model family, prompt, and repository; break out the academic test AI rewrites by generator and prompt. Keep repeated variants clustered by source identity in interpretation; do not report row-level confidence intervals or treat 228 variants as 228 independent source documents. Report test, validation, and human_only human flag fractions separately, without pooling them to hide a weak group.

The primary lexical-richness **advance screen** passes only if all of these prespecified conditions hold: at least 80% of technical `generate` variants have eligible source/variant pairs; at least 10% of eligible technical generations are flagged; no more than one of the 38 original technical documents is flagged; each of the three generator families flags at least 5% of its eligible variants; at least 15 of 20 repositories have a positive mean paired richness delta; and no more than 2% of eligible `human_only` academic papers are flagged. A pass would justify a new, separately frozen prospective corpus with verified human provenance and unseen generators. It would **not** qualify a deployed origin detector or a calibrated 1% FPR on software documentation. A failure retires direct reuse of the GEN threshold for that scope; do not flip feature direction, pick a new threshold, or select a favorable corpus after seeing results. Entropy and AI rewriting remain secondary descriptions, not substitute gates.

Preserve aggregate counts and input identities in the result, with no raw text. A verifier must reject a changed protocol, scorer, input hash, threshold, or saved result. This study does not change an Unswell runtime rule, score, editorial suggestion, or issue #349 qualification.
