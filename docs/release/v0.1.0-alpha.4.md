and [Action PR #7](https://github.com/stokaro/unswell-action/pull/7).
The four Homebrew archive hashes match GitHub's release records; all four native
installation jobs passed at `4683673a8e466cae876ba30d7b4829791324037c` in
[CI](https://github.com/stokaro/homebrew-unswell/actions/runs/34774795666).

All seven Action jobs passed at `d6de3d8cabea6bb6557ed5bcb8a13ee26500af8c`
in [CI](https://github.com/stokaro/unswell-action/actions/runs/34774800094).
The tested merge has the same tree. Nine retained public-release consumer
reports cover pass, policy failure, and incomplete input on the three platforms.
The runner-annotation artifacts match 27 annotations fetched from GitHub.
Those annotation fixtures use their pinned older CLI; the nine separate
consumer reports use alpha.4.

Both updates require a maintainer review before merging. Their main-branch
checks and the new Action version tag remain pending. The release consumers
above already support explicitly selecting alpha.4; they do not prove that the
Action's default or the tap's main formula has changed.

## Scan cost and scope

[Repeated measurements](../performance/alpha4/README.md) cover the original
four corpora with two CPUs of quota and a 512 MiB limit on diabolocom. All 24
scans complete within the resource budgets. The three larger corpora exceed
100,000 prose words; date-fns remains below that target. Changes in extracted
prose coverage are recorded alongside the original source identities.

This evidence does not qualify rule precision or a probability model.
Human qualification and final race, fuzzing, and coverage checks remain deferred
under their existing roadmap decisions.
